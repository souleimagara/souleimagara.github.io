How I work

A few things about the way I approach the work.

Learning the platform, not waiting on it

When Unity cannot reach something, I go and learn the platform rather than wait for someone else to. That is how C# turned into Kotlin, Swift and C++ along the way.

Documentation is part of the job

I wrote the documentation, the tutorials and the walkthrough videos for the SDK I work on. The aim is a studio getting it running without needing to message me.

Work that gets used

Most of it went into real use: a hospital, a national electoral authority, museums, and studios shipping to their players.

ZBD

Device & app attestation

2023 – present

Proving a device is genuine before it can earn. Keys are generated inside the hardware itself, in the Secure Enclave on iOS and a StrongBox-backed keystore on Android, so a modified client, an emulator or a rooted phone cannot claim rewards. Every reward and progress call is signed with a key that never leaves the chip.

  • Swift
  • Kotlin
  • C#
