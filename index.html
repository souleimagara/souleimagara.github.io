Who I am

How I work

A few things about the way I approach the work.

Learning the platform, not waiting on it

When Unity cannot do something directly, I learn the platform and find a way to make it work. That is how my work expanded from C# into Kotlin, Swift and C++.

Documentation is part of the job

I wrote the documentation, the tutorials and the walkthrough videos for the SDK I work on. The aim is a studio getting it running without needing to message me.

Work that gets used

Most of it went into real use: a hospital, a national electoral authority, museums, and studios shipping to their players.

ZBD

Device & app attestation

2023 – present

Proving a device is genuine before it can earn, which helps prevent reward abuse. I helped build the iOS and Android checks and built the Windows integration end to end.

This is a way of checking that a device is real and has not been tampered with before it is allowed to earn, which helps prevent reward abuse. People who try to farm rewards at scale use fake phones, modified devices and fake locations, and these checks help detect those attempts. I helped build the iOS and Android checks and built the Windows integration end to end, using the PC's built-in security chip and Windows Hello to prove the machine and the person are genuine. Together they make one trust system that covers both phones and PCs.

  • Swift
  • Kotlin
  • C#
  • TPM 2.0
  • Windows

Underlit Studio

AR and VR tour of the M’ghira 3 industrial zone

2025

At Underlit Studio, I built two apps for The Industrial Land Agency (AFI). Visitors can explore an animated 3D map of M’ghira 3 on their phone or step inside three factories on Meta Quest.

In the iOS and Android app, Vuforia tracks a flat surface so visitors can place the animated map on a table or floor. They tap points on the map to watch videos and learn what the factories produce.

On Meta Quest, visitors select one of three locations on the same map and teleport into the factory. Inside, they try a task based on the work done there, such as connecting cables or printing an aircraft part.

AFI uses the apps at fairs and conferences to show prospective investors which industries already operate in M’ghira 3 and what the zone can offer their businesses.
